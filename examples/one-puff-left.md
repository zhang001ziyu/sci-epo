# One Puff Left

An original reference script for a 75-second cartoon. This is a written example for the planned generator.

**Setting** — The night shift in a zero-gravity orbital mailroom.

**Cast** — Pip, a courier robot with one jetpack burst, and the station's oldest camera, on duty even on its birthday.

### 1 · Return receipt · 15 seconds

**Action.** A birthday cake eases out of its tin. Pip catches the heavy metal lid under one elbow. The cake sails past her other hand, icing first. At the delivery hatch, an old camera in a paper party hat swivels to watch.

**CAMERA:** “Will that require a signature?”

**PIP:** “Let me catch it first.”

**Shot.** Hold on the one lit fuel dot between Pip's shoulder blades. Rack focus to the cake drifting toward a spinning fan.

### 2 · Express delivery · 25 seconds

**Action.** A single puff sends Pip after the cake. She hooks the cardboard base with one finger just before the icing hits the fan guard. Pip and the cake coast away from the delivery hatch. The lid stays wedged under her elbow.

**PIP:** “One birthday cake. Almost sliced.”

**Shot.** Match the round cake to the round gauge on Pip's back. Its last dot goes dark. Let the fan wind down in the silence.

### 3 · Proof of delivery · 35 seconds

**Action.** Pip looks at the dead gauge, then at the lid. She throws the lid away from the hatch. The recoil sends her and the cake slowly back toward the waiting camera. A dab of icing catches on its lens like a white moustache.

**CAMERA:** “Face does not match employee photo.”

**Action.** Pip looks from the camera to the little employee portrait taped beside it. She paints the same moustache on the portrait. The hatch light turns green.

**PIP:** “Happy birthday.”

**Shot.** End on the camera, the portrait and their matching crooked moustaches. The lid pings somewhere offscreen.

### Revision exercise

Lock scenes 1 and 2. Ask for a funnier ending. A candidate that reads “Pip fires her jetpack again and rockets back to the hatch” conflicts with the last fuel dot going dark in scene 2. The lid ending gives the animator a different physical action and pays off something visible in the first scene.
